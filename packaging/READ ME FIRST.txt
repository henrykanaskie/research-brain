research brain: installing it
=============================

This folder has everything you need. You don't need Python, GitHub or anything else.

MAC
1. Double-click "Install-research-brain.command".
2. If the Mac says it "can't be opened" or "Apple could not verify" it: open System Settings >
   Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway" next to Install-research-brain.command,
   then double-click it again.
   Still blocked? Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities), type  bash  and a space, drag
   Install-research-brain.command into the Terminal window, and press Return.
3. A window shows the install (a few minutes the first time). When it says Done, research brain
   opens. From then on, open it from Launchpad or your Applications folder.

WINDOWS
1. Double-click "Install-research-brain.cmd".
2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. When it says Done, research brain opens. From then on, open it from the Start menu.

AFTERWARDS
- You can delete this folder (and the zip it came in). Your library lives in Documents > research brain.
- Chrome extension (optional): unzip research-brain-extension-....zip into a folder you keep (for
  example in Documents), then in Chrome go to chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click
  "Load unpacked" and choose that folder.
- Updates: when your lab shares a newer version, download it and run its installer again, the same
  way. Your library stays exactly as it is.
- Everything else (adding papers, the AI, free paper-lookup keys, a first-ten-minutes tour, and how
  to report a problem) is in README.md in this folder: open it with any text editor or a browser.
