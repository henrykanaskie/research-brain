research brain: installing it
=============================

You need about 700 MB of free space and an internet connection. Nothing else.

WINDOWS
1. Right-click the zip > "Extract All..." > Extract. (The installer won't run from inside the zip.)
2. Double-click "Install-research-brain.cmd". If Windows says "Windows protected your PC",
   click "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. When it says Done, research brain opens. Later, open it from the Start menu.
4. Chrome extension (optional): extract research-brain-extension-....zip to a folder outside
   OneDrive, such as C:\research brain extension. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions, turn on
   Developer mode, click "Load unpacked" and choose that folder.
If it's blocked on a university PC, take a screenshot (see REPORTING A PROBLEM).

MAC
1. Double-click the zip, then open the folder it makes.
2. Double-click "Install-research-brain.command". If the Mac says it "can't be opened":
   System Settings > Privacy & Security > "Open Anyway", then double-click it again.
   Still blocked? In Terminal, type  bash  and a space, drag the installer in, press Return.
3. When it says Done, research brain opens. Later, open it from Launchpad or Applications.
4. Chrome extension (optional): double-click research-brain-extension-....zip and move the folder
   into your home folder. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click
   "Load unpacked" and choose that folder.

LINUX
1. Unzip, open a terminal in the folder, and run:  bash install.sh
2. Later, open it from your app menu.
3. Chrome extension (optional): unzip research-brain-extension-....zip into your home folder, then
   load it in chrome://extensions as above.

AFTERWARDS
- You can delete this folder and the zip. Your library is in Documents > research brain.
- AI is optional (Gemini is free). Click the AI name at the top of the window to set one up.
- Updating: when there's a new version, click Update at the top of the window. You don't need a
  new zip. Your library stays as it is. Your version is in Settings > About.
- More (adding papers, free lookup keys, a tour) is in README.md in this folder.

REPORTING A PROBLEM
Send what you did, a screenshot, your version (Library settings), and the end of app.log:
  Windows: paste  %APPDATA%\research_brain  into File Explorer's address bar
  Mac:     ~/Library/Application Support/research_brain/app.log
  Linux:   ~/.local/share/research_brain/app.log
Never send API keys or passwords.
